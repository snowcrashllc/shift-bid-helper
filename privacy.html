Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snow Crash, LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") handles information related to Shift Bid Helper License and the Shift Bid Helper Chrome extension (collectively, the "Product").

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary to provide and manage the Product.

Information you provide through purchases or support

If you purchase a license, contact us, or request support, we may receive information such as your name, email address, order details, license status, and the contents of your message.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy or another authorized payment provider. The Shift Bid Helper extension does not collect or store credit card numbers or full payment card details. Payment processors may provide us with limited transaction, subscription, customer, and license information needed to manage your purchase.

Extension license information

The Shift Bid Helper extension processes the checkout email address and license key entered by the user for the purpose of activating, validating, refreshing, or deactivating a license.

Extension settings

The Shift Bid Helper extension stores settings or configuration values locally in your browser so the extension can function. This may include timing settings, shift index, selected location, manual scheduling options, license status, and similar operational settings.

Supported website interaction

When the user runs Shift Bid Helper, the extension reads and interacts with elements of the supported shift-bidding website as necessary to perform the user-requested shift-bidding workflow. This information is used only to provide the extension's functionality and is not collected for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

2. Information We Do Not Intend to Collect

The Product is not intended to collect health information, government identification numbers, financial account numbers, full payment card details, personal communications, or unrelated browsing history.

The Product does not collect, use, or sell user data for advertising purposes, profiling, or other purposes unrelated to providing and operating Shift Bid Helper.
